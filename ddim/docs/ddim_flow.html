01 · Big picture

One network.
Two processes.

Training teaches the U-Net to identify noise at arbitrary timesteps. Sampling repeatedly calls that same network, but DDIM chooses a new reverse transition that can skip most of the original 1,000 steps.

02 · Noise predictor

Inside εθ

The model is a time-conditioned U-Net. The encoder compresses spatial detail, the bottleneck mixes global context, and the decoder reconstructs a full-resolution noise field using skip connections.

03 · Reverse step

One DDIM update

At every selected timestep, the U-Net predicts noise. The sampler converts that prediction into a clean-image estimate and blends it into the next, less noisy state. η decides whether fresh randomness is added.

The mental model to keep.

01
Training learns noise, not images directly.

The U-Net sees a corrupted image and timestep, then predicts the exact noise used to corrupt it.

02
DDIM is mainly a sampler.

It changes how predictions are turned into the next state; the learned network and training loss stay the same.

03
η and step count are separate controls.

η controls randomness. The selected timestep subsequence controls compute and trajectory resolution.
